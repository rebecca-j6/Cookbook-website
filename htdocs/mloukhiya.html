Molokhiya

Cooktime: 30-50 mins | Preptime: 15 mins | Duration: 45-65 mins | Difficulty: Medium

Flavorful and Nutritious

Molokhiya, a dish made from finely chopped leaves of the jute plant, is a staple in Middle Eastern cuisine. It's often served with rice and chicken or beef, making it a hearty and comforting meal.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb frozen molokhiya leaves (finely chopped)
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 5 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 whole chicken, cut into pieces
  • 1 tbsp ground coriander
  • 1 tbsp butter or ghee
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • Lemon wedges (for serving)
  • Cooked white rice (for serving)

Instructions

  1. In a large pot, cook the chicken pieces with water, salt, and pepper until fully cooked. Remove the chicken, and set the broth aside.
  2. In a separate pan, melt the butter or ghee and sauté the onion until translucent.
  3. Add the minced garlic and ground coriander, cooking until fragrant.
  4. Add the frozen molokhiya leaves and stir well.
  5. Slowly pour in the chicken broth, stirring until the mixture is well combined. Let it simmer for 10-15 minutes.
  6. Serve the molokhiya hot with rice and lemon wedges, along with the cooked chicken on the side.

Tips & Tricks

To enhance the flavor, you can add a splash of lemon juice while cooking the molokhiya. For a richer taste, use ghee instead of butter.
