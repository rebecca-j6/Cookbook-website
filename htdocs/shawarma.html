Chicken Shawarma

Cooktime: 60 mins | Preptime: 120 mins | Duration: 180 mins | Difficulty: Medium

Juicy and Flavorful

Shawarma is a popular Middle Eastern street food, made with marinated chicken, beef, or lamb, cooked to perfection and wrapped in pita bread. This homemade recipe will bring the street flavors to your kitchen.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken thighs (boneless, skinless), sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 4 pita bread wraps
  • Chopped lettuce, tomatoes, and onions for serving
  • Tahini sauce or garlic sauce for drizzling

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, mix minced garlic, yogurt, olive oil, vinegar, cumin, paprika, turmeric, cinnamon, salt, and pepper.
  2. Add the sliced chicken thighs to the marinade and coat well. Let it marinate for at least 1 hour (or overnight for best results).
  3. Preheat a grill or pan over medium heat. Cook the marinated chicken until fully cooked, about 5-7 minutes per side.
  4. Remove from heat and let the chicken rest for a few minutes before slicing into thin strips.
  5. Warm the pita bread wraps and fill them with the sliced chicken, lettuce, tomatoes, and onions.
  6. Drizzle with tahini sauce or garlic sauce, wrap tightly, and serve hot.

Tips & Tricks

For extra flavor, serve your Shawarma with pickles and fries. You can also make a quick garlic sauce by blending mayonnaise, garlic, lemon juice, and a pinch of salt.
